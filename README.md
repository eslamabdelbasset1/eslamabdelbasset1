## 👨‍💻 &nbsp;About Me & Engineering Profile

I am a **Senior Backend Developer & Full Stack Engineer** with over **5 years of experience** specializing in the design, architecture, and deployment of mission-critical web platforms, multi-tenant SaaS applications, and enterprise ERP solutions. My focus is on writing clean, modular, and test-driven code that scales under high concurrent load.

- 🚀 **Backend Specialization:** Expertise in **PHP 8+** and the **Laravel ecosystem**, alongside **Node.js, NestJS, and Express.js** for event-driven microservices.
- ⚡ **Database Tuning:** Proven success in **boosting database query performance by 40%**, restructuring schema indexes, and orchestrating distributed **Redis** caching tiers.
- 🛡️ **Architecture & Standards:** Strong advocate of **Domain-Driven Design (DDD)**, Clean Architecture, SOLID design patterns, CQRS, and automated testing (PHPUnit/Pest).
- 📜 **Regulatory Compliance:** Hands-on implementation of enterprise integrations, including **ZATCA Phase 2 E-Invoicing** compliance for KSA/GCC markets.
- 🎓 **Education:** Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science from *Modern Academy for Science and Technology*.
